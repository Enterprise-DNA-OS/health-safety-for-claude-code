---
description: Put a conversation, a decision or a scene fact on the record - against an incident, a hazard, a site or a person - the moment it happens.
---

1. Run `npm run safety -- log <target> "what was said or done" --by=<who>` where the target is an incident ref (INC-201), a hazard ref (HAZ-103), a site or a person.
2. Log at the moment, not at the end of the day: scene facts on a notifiable event ("taped off, photos taken, family called") become the WorkSafe notification draft and the investigation record.
3. Decisions get logged with their reason: an action re-dated, a hazard accepted at its current control level, a contractor allowed on supervised. The log is where "why" lives.
4. The log is evidence of engagement too: the conversation with the crew about the near miss is worth one line here.
